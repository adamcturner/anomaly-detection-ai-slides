Threat Monitoring and Hunting: AI Opportunities

Both capabilities run without AI today. Two opportunities widen what AI examines in the backup

Today

No AI

Known-indicator matching against backup data.

  • Threat intelligence feeds supply hashes and YARA rules
  • Monitoring scans backups for those IOCs automatically
  • Hunting searches backup history for the same indicators
Files examined by AI
Opportunity 1

AI file targeting

AI picks which files and paths deserve a scan or a hunt.

  • Prioritizes files by location, type and recent change
  • Narrows scan scope on each snapshot
  • Shortens time to a verdict across many snapshots
Files examined by AI
Opportunity 2

AI analysis of the whole OS

AI reviews the full operating system image inside the backup.

  • Examines system binaries, services, configuration and persistence points
  • Can surface threats that have no known signature
  • Supports a verdict on whether a recovery point is clean
